Privacy Policy

SYK Studio is committed to transparent, privacy-respecting practices. We do not sell data, track you across the web, or load third-party ad surveillance.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide to us when contacting our studio or requesting a project estimate via email. This includes your name, email address, company details, and project requirements. We never collect sensitive personal information without your explicit permission.

2. How We Use Your Information

Any information shared with SYK Studio is strictly used to:

  • Respond to your direct inquiries and project consultations.
  • Prepare project scopes, architectural roadmaps, and commercial proposals.
  • Coordinate milestones, communication, and ongoing website maintenance contracts.

3. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses local preferences solely to remember your cookie consent choice. We do not run third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or invasive data brokers. You have complete control to accept or reject preferences using our consent banner.

4. Data Security

We take appropriate technical measures to prevent unauthorized access, accidental alteration, or disclosure of communications sent to our studio.

5. Contacting Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to have your details updated or deleted from our records, please contact us directly at syk.studio4@gmail.com.
